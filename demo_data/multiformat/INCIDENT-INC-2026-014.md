# Incident Report INC-2026-014 — Pump P-102A Bearing Failure

## Summary
On 2026-01-13, pump P-102A tripped on high bearing temperature (TE-202). Root cause was bearing seizure (FM-BRG-01).

## Contributing Factors
- Missed monthly bearing lubrication.
- Vibration trend not reviewed.

## Corrective Actions
Bearing replaced under WO-99201; lubrication interval added to the preventive schedule; vibration alarm threshold lowered.

## Lessons Learned
Review vibration trends weekly; near-miss on adjacent pump P-102B suggests a fleet-wide lubrication audit.
